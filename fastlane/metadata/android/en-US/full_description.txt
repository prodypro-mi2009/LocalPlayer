<b>Your music. Your files. Nothing else.</b>

LocalPlayer plays the audio files already on your phone: MP3, FLAC, M4A and AAC. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. Network features exist only when you start them yourself.

<b>Playback</b>
<ul>
<li>Queue with Play next, Add to queue and undo</li>
<li>Sleep timer with fade-out, equalizer and ReplayGain</li>
<li>Home-screen widgets and media notification</li>
<li>Continue where you left off (optional)</li>
</ul>

<b>Library</b>
<ul>
<li>Songs, albums, artists, genres, years, playlists, favourites and history</li>
<li>Import and export playlists as M3U</li>
<li>Duplicate finder (files are never deleted)</li>
<li>Statistics and an automatic mix built from your listening</li>
</ul>

<b>Tags and lyrics</b>
<ul>
<li>Edit tags and cover art directly in the file, verified after saving</li>
<li>Synchronised (LRC) and plain lyrics, with an editor and lyrics search</li>
<li>Current lyric line above the progress bar with a colour fill (optional)</li>
</ul>

<b>Look and feel</b>
<ul>
<li>Light, dark and dynamic colour, backgrounds made from the album art, custom fonts</li>
<li>English and Greek</li>
</ul>

<b>Backup</b>
<ul>
<li>One backup file with settings, playlists, favourites, history and statistics</li>
<li>Choose what to restore; optional weekly automatic backup (off by default)</li>
</ul>

<b>Optional online features</b> (all started by you):
<ul>
<li>Lyrics search</li>
<li>Playlist from a public Spotify link, with the missing songs looked up on YouTube (no Spotify login)</li>
<li>Download from YouTube with yt-dlp, only for content you are allowed to download</li>
<li>Update check from GitHub releases (off by default)</li>
</ul>

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. ARM64 devices.
